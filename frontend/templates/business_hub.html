TOOLS FOR LOCAL ENTREPRENEURS

Grow your business with confidence.

Simple, practical starting points for reaching customers, managing cash, and building a trusted local business.

Market your business online

Keep your business name, phone, location, opening hours, and photos consistent across your MtaaBiz profile and social accounts. Ask happy customers to share your listing.

Attract and retain customers

Reply to enquiries promptly, explain prices clearly, and make it easy to contact you. A helpful follow-up can turn a first-time customer into a regular.

Manage business finances

Record sales and expenses separately from personal spending. Review cash flow regularly, keep payment evidence, and plan for taxes and upcoming costs.

Register and formalize

Business registration requirements depend on the business type. Check current guidance from Kenya’s official eCitizen and Business Registration Service before submitting documents or fees.

Use social media effectively

Share useful updates on a schedule you can maintain. Include your location and contact details, use real product photos, and respond respectfully to questions.

Run a reliable operation

Write down regular tasks, stock levels, delivery arrangements, and service standards. Small routines make it easier to serve customers consistently.
